Dear all,

Please find below the arrangements for the final project in DASE7506 Advanced Machine Learning Methods.
- Topic: You may choose any topic related to the course, including large language models, reasoning models, or LLM agents.
- Team size: You may work individually or in a group of up to five students.
- Deliverable: Submit a written report describing your problem, approach, results, and limitations, following the style of a short conference or workshop paper.
- Template: You may use the IEEE Conference Paper Templates, available in both LaTeX and Word, as a formatting reference.
- Presentation: No oral presentation is required.
- Deadline: December 10, 2026.
- Weighting: The final project accounts for 30% of your final course grade.
Projects will be assessed on the novelty of the problem formulation or the soundness of the technical execution, as well as the clarity and quality of the written report.

If you have any questions about your proposed topic or the project requirements, please contact YANG Zhen (u3014008@connect.hku.hk
